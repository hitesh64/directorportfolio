Sneh Precast ConstroSolutions History (2008 – 2025)

The year-by-year milestone journey of precision, innovation, and scaling into Maharashtra's largest block manufacturer.

2008

Foundation of Sneh Precast

Founded by Mr. Pravin Chaudhari to serve India's precast infrastructure industry. Built on precision, innovation, and sustainability to support highways, metro networks, and smart cities.

2009

Semi-Automatic Plant & Glossy Pavers

Installed the first semi-automatic paving block plant. Introduced glossy pavers and designer tiles, significantly improving product finish and durability.

2010 – 2012

Infrastructure Alignment & Operational Efficiency

Focused on meeting complex infrastructure needs while reinforcing trust with clients, partners, and stakeholders through continuous process improvement.

2013 – 2014

Automatic Machinery Expansion

Installed automatic machinery in production units for paver and concrete blocks (2013), followed by a second automatic machine in 2014 to streamline public and private sector projects.

2015 – 2017

Advanced Curing, Shotblasting & Delivery Systems

Adopted early strength concrete curing (2015), implemented mechanized material handling delivery fleets (2016), and installed shotblasting machinery (2017) for superior surface finish.

2018 – 2019

Samruddhi Expressway Landmark Project

Increased capacity with 3 automatic block machines (2018). In 2019, executed first major on-site project with L&T for the Samruddhi Expressway, casting & erecting 60,000 RMT of pre-stressed boundary walls.

2020

Major Infra Contracts & Retaining Walls

Introduced Sneh Classic U Drains. Secured Samruddhi Expressway orders from Megha Engineering, Gayatri Projects, & Afcons Ltd. Partnered with Shinde Developers for Solapur–Vijapur highway RE blocks.

2021 – 2022

European Vacuum Wet Tech & Dry Mix Plant

Began vertical Hume pipe casting & Dry Mix Mortar Plant operations (2021). Integrated European hydraulic kerb stone vacuum wet concrete tech, Kraft Curing system, and 20 TPH Dry Mix line (2022).

2023 – 2024

Nere Facility & Land Acquisition

Launched Composite Panel Unit & Sand Plant (2023). Operationalized Nere facility, cast Precast Pedestals for a leading IT company, and acquired expansion land at Bhavadi (2024).

2025 • 40 Acres Expansion

WIL EL MIL Machines & Nationwide Leadership

Became the first in India to receive WIL EL MIL machines for large-size flagstones & kerb stones. Expanded across 40 acres of manufacturing space with specialised L Retaining Walls & U Drains.
